🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

The short version

  • We collect what you enter to run Rental Genie, and we don't sell it or use it for advertising.
  • When you use an AI feature, such as Snap it for receipts and leases, the document or text involved is sent to Anthropic, our AI provider, to read it or draft text.
  • Tenants only see their own lease, payments, documents, requests, and messages.
  • We don't currently process payments or store bank account or card numbers.
  • You can ask us to delete your data at any time.

1. Who we are

Rental Genie is operated by [LEGAL ENTITY NAME], a [STATE] limited liability company ("Rental Genie," "we," "us"). This policy explains what information we collect through the Rental Genie website and app, how we use it, and the choices you have.

2. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to:

  • Landlords and their team members who create an account to manage properties.
  • Tenants who are invited by a landlord to use the tenant portal.
  • Visitors to our public website.

3. Information we collect

Information landlords provide

  • Account details, such as your name, email address, company name, and password. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and stored in hashed form, not as readable text.
  • Property details, such as addresses, units, and descriptions.
  • Financial records you enter, including rent and payment history, expenses, mortgage and escrow activity, property tax, insurance, and HOA information.
  • Lease information and documents you upload.
  • Information about your tenants, such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, and lease terms.
  • Maintenance requests, messages, and support requests.
  • Receipts, invoices, leases and other documents you snap or upload, and payment-alert emails you forward to Rental Genie if you set up forwarding.

Information tenants provide

  • Account details, such as name, email address, and phone number.
  • Maintenance requests, including descriptions and any attachments.
  • Messages sent to your landlord through the portal.
  • Preferences you save in your tenant account.

Information collected automatically

  • Technical information such as IP address, browser type, and device type, which our hosting and security providers record in server logs.
  • Information stored in your browser's local storage to keep you signed in and remember your selections (see section 10).

We do not currently process rent payments, and we do not collect or store bank account numbers, routing numbers, or card numbers. If we add online payments, we will update this policy before that feature launches.

4. How we use information

We use information to:

  • Provide the service, including calculating reports such as net operating income, cap rate, and cash-on-cash return.
  • Read documents and payment alerts, and draft listing descriptions, when you use an AI feature.
  • Send service emails, such as sign-in links, tenant invitations, and maintenance request acknowledgments.
  • Respond to support requests.
  • Keep the service secure, prevent abuse, and fix problems.
  • Understand how the service is used so we can improve it.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for targeted advertising.

5. AI processing

Rental Genie uses AI models provided by Anthropic for these features:

  • Snap it. When you snap a receipt or upload a signed lease to fill in a form, we send that file and the names and addresses of your properties to Anthropic, which reads it and returns the details. You review them, and nothing is saved until you choose to save.
  • Listing descriptions. When you ask for an AI-drafted listing description, we send the property facts shown on that page.
  • Payment alerts. If you set up forwarding of payment notifications (for example from Zelle or Venmo), our email provider receives those emails, and unusual formats may be sent to Anthropic to read the amount and sender. You confirm each payment before it is recorded.
  • Ask Genie (not yet available). When it launches, your question and the records needed to answer it will be sent to Anthropic.

Anthropic handles this data as our service provider under its commercial terms, which do not allow it to use our customers' data to train its models. You can read more in Anthropic's privacy policy.

These features cannot change your records or contact anyone on their own; you review and save the results. Tenants do not use them. If you prefer that your data not be processed this way, don't use the AI features; the rest of Rental Genie works without them.

6. How we share information

We share information only in these situations:

  • Service providers that help us run Rental Genie, under agreements that limit how they can use the data: Supabase (database, authentication, file storage, and server functions), Cloudflare (hosting and network security), Anthropic (AI features), our email providers (service emails and, if you set up forwarding, receiving payment alerts), and Google Fonts (which receives your IP address when a page loads its fonts).
  • Between landlords and tenants, as described in section 7.
  • Legal reasons, when required by law or legal process, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of our users, the public, or Rental Genie.
  • Business transfers, if Rental Genie is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets. We will notify you before your information becomes subject to a different privacy policy.
  • With your permission, in any other case.

7. Landlords, tenants, and who sees what

Landlords control the property, lease, and tenant records in their accounts. Landlords are responsible for having a lawful basis to enter tenant information into Rental Genie and for giving tenants any notices required by law.

Tenants can see only their own lease, payment records, documents, maintenance requests, and messages. Tenants cannot see a landlord's other properties, other tenants, or the landlord's financial records.

If you are a tenant and have a question about information your landlord entered, please contact your landlord first. You can also contact us, and we will help where we can.

8. Security

Your data is stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted by Supabase. Row-level security rules at the database level keep each account's records separate. Data is encrypted in transit using TLS and encrypted at rest by our hosting provider.

No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a security incident that affects your personal information, we will notify you as required by law.

9. How long we keep information

We keep your information while your account is active. If you ask us to delete your account, we will delete your information within 30 days, except where we need to keep certain records to meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, or enforce our agreements. Backup copies are removed on our providers' regular backup schedule.

When a landlord deletes their account, tenant records stored in that account are deleted as well.

10. Cookies and local storage

Rental Genie uses your browser's local storage to keep you signed in and to remember preferences such as your selected company and property. Cloudflare may set cookies needed for security and to protect the site from abuse. We do not use advertising cookies.

11. Your choices and rights

  • Access and update: You can view and update your account information in your account settings.
  • Delete: To delete your account and data, contact us. We will confirm your request and complete it within 30 days.
  • Copy: Landlords can export report data to CSV. To request a copy of other information we hold about you, contact us.
  • Emails: Service emails, such as sign-in links, are needed to use Rental Genie. If we ever send marketing emails, each will include a way to unsubscribe.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional privacy rights under state law. We respond to access, correction, and deletion requests from all users, wherever they live.

12. Children

Rental Genie is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make significant changes, we will notify account holders by email or in the app before the changes take effect, and we will update the date at the top of this page.

14. Contact us

For questions about this policy or your information, please contact us or write to us at [MAILING ADDRESS].
