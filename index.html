Straight answers.

Can my tenants pay rent online?

Not yet. Today you record each payment, including partial payments, and Rental Genie flags anything late. Online payments by bank transfer are the next feature we're building.

What can Ask Genie do?

Ask Genie isn't available yet. When it launches it will answer questions about the data in your account: rent, expenses, properties, and maintenance. It will read your records but won't change them or contact anyone. The AI feature available today is Snap it, which reads a receipt or lease and fills in the form for you to check.

Does this replace my accountant?

No. Rental Genie keeps your records organized by property and exports them to CSV, which makes your accountant's job faster. It doesn't file taxes or give tax advice.

Who can see my data?

Only you and the people you invite. Each account's records are separated at the database level, so one landlord can never see another's data. Tenants only see their own lease, payments, and requests.

What happens when early access ends?

The Free plan stays free for up to 3 properties. If you use Pro features, we'll email you before any billing starts, and you can choose whether to continue.
